Touch Controls

Steer with Tilt (lean the device), Swipe (drag on the left half of the screen) or on-screen Buttons. The car accelerates by itself. Hold Drift while steering to slide and fill the charge bar, tap Nitro to spend it, and tap Boost the moment the car straightens after a drift. Tap the pause button at the top to pause or reset your car.

Controls (PC client defaults)

↑Accelerate ↓Brake/Reverse ←→Steer Shift+Direction Drift Ctrl Nitro/Item Alt Swap items W Mini Boost R Reset C Camera M Music PgUp/PgDn Song Esc Pause
Tips: drifting fills the charge bar, and a full bar gives you 1 nitro (up to 2). After releasing Shift, tap ↑ the moment the car straightens to trigger a Mini Boost (the sooner the better; nail it for a “Perfect”). Stack a Mini Boost with Nitro for a “Double Boost”. Press ↑ the instant “GO” appears for a rocket start, and tap ↑ as you land from a jump for a Landing Boost. Blue arrows are boost pads.
