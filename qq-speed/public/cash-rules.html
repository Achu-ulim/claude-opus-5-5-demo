Cash race rules

Draft, last updated 10 October 2026

Cash races are online races for real money between players. They are only for players aged 18 or over, and they are completely separate from tokens.

Who can take part

  • You need a Driftbolt account and must confirm your date of birth: you must be 18 or over. It can't be changed afterwards.
  • Your cash wallet is in naira or US dollars, as you choose. You can change it only while it is empty.
  • Before your first withdrawal you verify your identity with your BVN or NIN. We keep only the result, the name on record and the date, never the number. Winnings are paid only to a bank account in that name.

Entries and the prize pool

  • The host picks the entry, the number of racers (2, 4 or 6), the prize split, the track and the laps. Everyone races the same car with the same setup, and items are off.
  • Your entry is taken from your cash balance when you create or join a room, and held in that room's pot. Nobody, including us, can use it while it is held.
  • If the room doesn't fill within 10 minutes, if a full room doesn't start within 3 minutes, or if the host calls it off before the start, every entry is refunded in full. A player who leaves before the start gets their entry back.
  • The prize pool is every entry. Our platform fee (currently 10%) comes off the pool; the rest is the prize. Winner takes all, or with 4 or more racers 60% / 30% / 10% to the top three. Racers on exactly the same time share the places they tie for equally. If fewer racers finish than there are prizes, the prizes nobody reached go to the winner.
  • If nobody finishes, every entry is refunded and no fee is taken.

Fair play

  • The result is checked by our server, not your device: checkpoints passed in order, lap times the track allows, speeds the car can reach, no jumps in position, and times in step with the server's clock.
  • A racer who disconnects for more than 30 seconds, or leaves during the race, doesn't finish and forfeits their entry. It stays in the prize pool.
  • If a check fails, the payout is frozen and a person reviews what the server recorded of the race. They either pay out as the result stands or refund every entry.
  • Winnings wait 10 minutes after the race before they are paid. During that time any racer in the room can report the race, which holds the payout for review.
  • Cheating, using more than one account, or racing in a group to fix a result can lead to an account being closed and its winnings held.

Withdrawals and converting to tokens

  • You can withdraw your available balance (prizes still in their report window come later) to your verified bank account through Paystack. There is a minimum withdrawal, and Paystack's transfer fee is taken from the amount. Withdrawals usually arrive within minutes; we email a receipt.
  • Some withdrawals are held for a short check first, for example straight after a deposit with no racing. The money is set aside from your balance meanwhile.
  • If a transfer fails or is reversed by the bank, the money goes back to your cash balance.
  • You can convert cash to tokens at a bonus rate. This is one-way: tokens can never be turned back into cash.

Play responsibly

  • Set your own daily deposit and entry limits in Winnings. Lowering a limit works at once; raising one takes 24 hours.
  • Take a break of 7, 30 or 90 days from cash races. During a break you can't enter or deposit, and it can't be ended early. You can still withdraw.
  • Only race with money you can afford to lose. If racing for money stops being fun, take a break.

Contact

Questions about a race, a payout or a withdrawal: support@driftbolt.online. Quote the room code or the payment reference.
